GAKU Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. GAKU Master is operated by Seito Sakamoto (GAKU Online Japanese). Contact: seitojapanese.online@gmail.com

What we collect

Your name, email address, country, native language, study goals and level, account status (trial, paid, GAKU student), a device identifier used to limit logins to approved devices, and consent records for our policy and payment steps. Study content you create (vocabulary, study sets, saved text) is stored mainly on your own device; some items you ask us to sync are stored in our database.

How we use it

To run your account, build your study plan, enforce the free trial and device limits, send account and service emails, and provide support. We do not sell your personal data and do not show third-party ads.

AI features and service providers

When you use features such as word lookup, translation, readings, or creating activities from text, the text you submit is sent to third-party AI providers (currently Groq and DeepInfra) only to produce the result. Please do not enter sensitive personal information. Other providers we use: Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (hosting), Brevo (email), and Stripe (web payments; we do not see or store your card number).

Microphone

If you choose to use listening or speaking features, the app asks for microphone access. Audio is used to produce text or feedback and is not kept as a recording by GAKU.

Retention and deletion

If a trial ends without payment or enrollment as a GAKU student, saved study data is reset after the grace period stated in the app. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (“Delete my account”), or by emailing us; we will delete your account data except records we must keep by law.

Children

GAKU Master is intended for people aged 13 and older.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Email us to make a request.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change.
