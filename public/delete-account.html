Delete Your GAKU Master Account

GAKU Master is operated by Seito Sakamoto (GAKU Online Japanese). This page explains how to delete your account and the data linked to it. See also our Privacy Policy.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

  1. Open GAKU Master and sign in.
  2. On the dashboard, tap Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation message and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, or you prefer email, write to seitojapanese.online@gmail.com from the email address you used to register. Use the subject “Delete my GAKU Master account” and we will delete your account.

What is deleted

Your account and the data linked to it: name, email address, country, native language, study goals and level, account status, device identifier, and the study content you asked us to sync to our database. Study content stored only on your own device is removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

We do not keep audio recordings. Voice audio sent for speech-to-text is not stored by GAKU.

What may be kept

Records we are required to keep by law (for example, payment and tax records). If you are enrolled as a GAKU student, the in-app confirmation message explains how your data is handled for that case.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store. Please cancel it in your Google Play or Apple ID subscription settings so you are not billed again.

Questions

Contact seitojapanese.online@gmail.com.
